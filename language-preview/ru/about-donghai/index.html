Our Sourcing Process

A systematic, data-driven approach to production coordination.

01

Specification Intake

We ingest your technical drawings (CAD/PDF) and define exact tolerances, material requirements, and optical parameters.

02

Workshop Allocation

The project is parsed and assigned to the specific workshop within our network optimized for your required cut and material.

03

Production Oversight

Our local agents conduct mid-cycle inspections at the workshops to ensure dimensional accuracy before the run is complete.

04

Final Metrology

Completed batches return to our central office for final QC using digital calipers and optical comparators before export.
